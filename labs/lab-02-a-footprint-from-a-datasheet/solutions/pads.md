# Pads for TI's RHL0024A

<!-- Reference solution for Lab 02, written by src/make_reference.py. -->

Every length in mm. "Long sides" are pins 2 to 11 and 14 to 23, whose pads point along x;
"short sides" are pins 1, 12, 13 and 24, whose pads point along y.

| Quantity | Long sides | Short sides |
|---|---|---|
| Z | 4.23 | 6.23 |
| G | 2.39 | 4.39 |
| X | 0.26 | 0.26 |
| Pad length | 0.89 | 0.89 |
| Pad centre | 1.67 | 2.67 |

Exposed pad: 2.05 x 4.05

At G, the inner end of each pad would be 0.17 mm from the exposed pad, less
than the 0.2 mm KLC F6.3 asks for, so the inner ends move out to 1.225 mm (long
sides) and 2.225 mm (short sides) from the centre, and the pads get shorter.
